Informal usability testing with creators and designers who actively create content.

Time to reach creative direction

2 days → 1 days

Primary drop-off

Templates (Users couldn’t relate or trust them)

Learnings

I introduced solutions before fully validating mental models
While users liked the outcomes, confusion around templates showed that I assumed how creators interpret AI-generated artifacts.

I over-indexed on feature completeness instead of clarity
Templates added value functionally, but increased cognitive load because users didn’t understand why they were seeing them.

I tested outcomes more than transitions
Testing focused on “Did this help?” but drop-offs happened between steps, especially from guidance → templates.

This phase taught me that designing with AI isn’t about adding intelligence it’s about removing uncertainty at the right moments.
